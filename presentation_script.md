## SLIDE 1 - TITLE (30 seconds)

> Hello professors and classmates.
>
> We are VoGiaCu Team, and today we present our UX/UI final project: **BNConcert**.
>
> BNConcert is an online concert ticketing app focused on making booking faster, clearer, and easier.

---

## SLIDE 2 - PROJECT OVERVIEW (1 minute)

> This project has four parts:
>
> **Purpose**: Design a better concert ticket booking experience.
>
> **Scope**: Authentication, browsing, seat selection, checkout, and dashboard.
>
> **Platform**: Responsive app experience for desktop and mobile.
>
> **Tools**: Figma, React Native, Python API, and JavaScript.

---

## SLIDE 3 - PROBLEM STATEMENT (1 minute)

> Our core problem statement is:
>
> "Current ticketing platforms are hard to navigate because of complex flows, unclear information, and fragmented payment experience."
>
> Key issues include confusing interfaces, missing seating and event details, too many booking steps, and slow design-to-code handoff.

---

## SLIDE 4 - PROJECT GOALS (1 minute)

> From that problem, we set four goals:
>
> - Simplify the booking flow.
> - Improve interface clarity and usability.
> - Keep delivery consistency between design and engineering.
> - Maintain accessibility standards across the product.

---

## SLIDE 5 - USER RESEARCH (1 minute)

> Research is based on three streams:
>
> - Survey feedback to capture recurring pain points.
> - Interview insights to validate user frustrations in real booking scenarios.
> - Competitor benchmarking to identify gaps and opportunities.
>
> Main insight: users need a shorter path from discovery to successful checkout with clearer information.

---

## SLIDE 6 - COMPETITIVE ANALYSIS (1 minute)

> We compared major international and Vietnam/Asia ticketing platforms.
>
> Common strengths are modern visual design and rich feature sets.
>
> Common weaknesses are complicated booking flows and limited personalization.
>
> That gap creates room for BNConcert.

---

## SLIDE 7 - PAIN POINTS (1 minute)

> We grouped pain points by severity:
>
> **High**: Long booking flow and insufficient event/seating information.
>
> **Medium**: Hard payment process and weak search/filter quality.
>
> **Low but important**: Accessibility gaps and limited reminder support.

---

## SLIDE 8 - USER PERSONA (1 minute)

> Our core persona is Minh Anh, a mobile-first concert goer.
>
> Goals:
> - Find matching concerts quickly.
> - Choose seats with confidence.
> - Complete payment without unnecessary friction.
>
> Frustrations:
> - Too many steps.
> - Unclear seat and venue information.
> - Uncertainty at confirmation stage.

---

## SLIDE 9 - USER JOURNEY MAP (1 minute)

> The journey includes five phases:
>
> **Discovery -> Details -> Seats -> Payment -> Confirmation**
>
> Emotional drop is strongest at payment.
>
> Priority opportunity: improve payment and confirmation clarity to raise completion confidence.

---

## SLIDE 10 - IDEATION & SOLUTION (1 minute)

> Our solution package includes:
>
> - Smart Recommend
> - Quick Book
> - Interactive Seat Map
> - Flexible Payments
> - E-Ticket QR
> - Social Share
>
> Together, these features target the major friction points in discovery, booking, and checkout.

---

## SLIDE 11 - ENGINEERING VALIDATION (1 minute)

>
> - Static checks for frontend and backend.
> - API schema and contract consistency checks.
> - Regression and smoke tests on critical flows.
>
> Current status is pre-production, so no public KPI or user score is reported.
